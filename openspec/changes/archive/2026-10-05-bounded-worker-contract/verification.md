Head-bound jobs. `lifecycle_workers.build_job` derives a job `{kind, number, head, task_identity, attempt}` from the coordinator's derived candidate state (the v2 handoff record's `next_job`, otherwise the state-to-kind map) for review, repair, integration-repair, finalize, retrospective, terminal-reconciliation and cleanup. `result_is_current` discards a result for a different head. Publishing review/repair/finalize jobs into handoff records is done by the coordinator children that own those transitions (pr-review-repair-gate onward); this change supplies the job/claim contract they use, `lifecycle_workers.job_record` and the coordinator entrypoint `publication_queue.publish_job`, which publishes an explicit `{kind, head, task_identity, attempt}` job through the existing handoff record without a new state; `build_job` prefers those explicit fields and offers nothing for a job published against an older head.

Claims. Claims are `dev-platform-lifecycle-claim:v1` PR comment markers `{job, worker, head, expires_at}`. `winning_claim` selects the earliest valid, unexpired, trusted (`candidate_lifecycle.trusted_marker_comment`) claim for the exact job and head by comment order; expired and head-stale claims are reclaimable; `work_next` posts its claim, re-reads, and abandons without side effects when it lost. The `work-next --repo --kinds [--dry-run]` CLI advertises job kinds, so local, remote and hybrid placement differ only in where it runs.

No credentials for LLM workers. `credential_free_env` removes GitHub-scoped tokens and API keys, askpass helpers and `SSH_AUTH_SOCK`, disables git credential helpers (`GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL`/`GIT_CONFIG_SYSTEM` to the null device, `GIT_CONFIG_NOSYSTEM=1`, `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0`) and keeps the LLM provider's own key; `run_llm` runs with that environment and `stdin=DEVNULL` in a disposable checkout prepared through `disposable_repository_sandbox`. `execute_job` (and `work-next --run`) runs the claimed job end to end: the LLM works only in a disposable checkout at the job head; a review must leave HEAD and the worktree unchanged and has no push path; a write result is discarded with a recorded `discarded: head moved` result when the PR head moved, otherwise it is fetched alone into a fresh harness-owned clone and validated and pushed there with hooks and fsmonitor disabled, so hooks, remotes and credential settings planted in the LLM checkout are never consulted; every outcome posts a `dev-platform-lifecycle-result:v1` marker. `work_next` also abandons a won claim when the PR head moved meanwhile. `validate_worker_result` accepts only a fast-forward from the expected head within the candidate's allowed paths, rejects workflow and lifecycle-evidence edits, and rejects any write for review jobs; `push_validated` is the only push, guarded by the expected-head lease.
