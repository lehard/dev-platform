# Proposal: Requirement evidence in Process Health Review

## Why

The weekly process lens currently names managed tasks, PR/lifecycle failures and open process issues, but does not explicitly inspect where in the Requirement-first path a finding arose. Early and cross-child symptoms can be lost when all technical children complete cleanly.

## What changes

Expand the bounded review contract to inspect relevant Requirement-level and pre-authoring process evidence, classify it with existing issue and managed-work context, and group symptoms by likely root cause. Preserve advisory, read-only behavior and the existing one-report surface.

## Success evidence

A representative case with successful child changes and an early or cross-child process finding appears in the review's bounded evidence and root-cause analysis. Current-context and duplicate-work safeguards remain intact.

## Constraints

Consume existing friction/process-issue evidence; do not add storage, scheduling, or task automation.
